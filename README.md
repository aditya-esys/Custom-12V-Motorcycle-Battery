# Custom 12V Motorcycle Battery Pack 🏍️⚡

A custom-built 12V battery pack designed from scratch to replace traditional lead-acid batteries and reliably power the electrical starter system of a Hero Glamour motorcycle.

## 🛠️ Components & Engineering Details
* **Power Source:** 4 x 32700 Lithium batteries connected in series to provide a stable 12V output.
* **Battery Management System:** Integrated a heavy-duty **4S BMS** to safely handle the high-current draw during engine ignition and protect the cells from overcharging/overdischarging.
* **Wiring:** Used heavy-gauge (thick) wires for all input and output connections to prevent heating and ensure maximum power delivery to the starter motor.
* **Custom Enclosure:** Designed and crafted a lightweight, shock-resistant custom casing using **Sunboard** to hold the cells tightly and protect the internal circuitry.

## 🎯 Project Objective
This project demonstrates practical skills in power electronics, high-current battery pack assembly, and custom hardware packaging for real-world automotive applications.

# Custom 12V Lithium-Ion Motorcycle Battery Pack 🏍️⚡

A custom-engineered 12V lithium-ion battery pack designed from scratch to replace traditional lead-acid batteries and reliably power the electrical starter system of a Hero Glamour motorcycle.

## 🛠️ Engineering Details & Integration
* **Cell Configuration:** High-discharge lithium-ion cells configured to deliver stable 12V output required for automotive starter motors.
* **Battery Management System (BMS):** Integrated a heavy-duty BMS with multiple MOSFETs to handle high-current draw safely during engine ignition and provide overcharge/discharge protection.
* **Safety & Relay System:** Implemented an XH-M609 protection module and relay system to ensure optimal voltage regulation and prevent battery degradation.
* **Custom Enclosure:** Designed and built a lightweight, shock-resistant custom white casing to protect the internal circuitry from vibrations and weather elements, with neatly routed thick gauge input/output wiring.

## 🎯 Project Objective
This project demonstrates practical skills in power electronics, battery balancing, high-current circuit protection, and custom hardware packaging for real-world automotive applications.
